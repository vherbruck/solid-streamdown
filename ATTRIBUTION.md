Framework-agnostic modules (block splitting, preprocessors, remark and rehype
plugins) are ported from Streamdown, adapted for SolidJS's reactive runtime.
Each ported file's header names its upstream path and commit. See
`UPSTREAM.md` for the synced commit and a list of deviations, and `NOTICE`
for the notice that must travel with redistributions.
